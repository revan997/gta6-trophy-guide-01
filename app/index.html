    Kind

      Select a trophy to read its guide and sources.

      Three stages, built from the official descriptions: finish the story, clear the 100% checklist, then mop up what is left. PowerPyx puts the whole run at about 200 hours and difficulty 4/10, with one playthrough and no online trophies.

        Only one trophy can be missed, and even it is not confirmed to be required. Everything else stays available in free-roam after the story.

          Watch out while you play

          • Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them on your platinum save.
          • There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots).
          • The Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent and makes police far more aggressive.
          • Some optional missions expire, but none of them is needed for a trophy.

          Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; GTASpotter — GTA VI trophies and achievements.

          What to do next, given what you have already earned. The planner always puts the one missable trophy first while Chapter 1 is still open, then works through the stages in order.
